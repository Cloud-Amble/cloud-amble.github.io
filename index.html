· About Me

I'm Raina, a product manager focused on B-side operation backends and AI products. My way of working is simple: read people and break down needs with data first, then act.

I've built private-domain user segmentation and recall, SMS automation, and platform-coin group-buy campaigns, and contributed to AI features for a university student-affairs system.
